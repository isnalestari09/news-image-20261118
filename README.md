# Sum up 18 November's world news in one image

Entry for make.fun bounty `news-image-20261118` — $250, 50 places paid.
Deadline: Fri 20 Nov 2026 07:00 WIB (Fri 20 Nov 2026 00:00 UTC).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `output/entry.png` (or `/output/entry.png` when that directory exists).
Python 3.13, standard library only, no network, deterministic — two runs
produce identical bytes.

## The image

A 1024x1024 newspaper front page, "THE WORLD TODAY", dated
18 November 2026, No. 18, Page 1. Lead story: Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin
meet in Shenzhen ahead of trilateral talks with Donald Trump. Also covered:
the Gulf escalation (US strikes on Qeshm Island, Iran drones at Bahrain and
Kuwait), the Kerch / Crimea bridge partially collapsing, Mary Peltola winning
Alaska as Democrats take the US Senate 56-44, Ebola in DR Congo, the US losing
its measles-free status, a Russian military plane crash in Siberia (19 dead),
Gaza cold in displacement tents, and markets falling. Every story has its own
drawn illustration; no photographs, no ads, no other date.
